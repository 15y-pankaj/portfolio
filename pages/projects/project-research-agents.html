01 / CONTEXT

The opportunity.

A single long request to an AI rarely gives a dependable research report. Splitting the job between specialists makes every step easier to control and to check.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

Where I came in.

I designed the team structure, wrote separate instructions for each agent, and built the review loop and the hand-offs between steps.

03 / THE WORK IN PRACTICE

What got built.

Four specialists

One agent searches, one gathers the facts, one writes the report and one acts as a critic. Each has its own instructions.

A built-in reviewer

The critic scores every draft against quality criteria, so weak reports are caught before they reach the reader.

Consistent results

Each step passes on only what the next step needs, and the AI model runs at temperature 0, so the same topic gives consistent reports.
